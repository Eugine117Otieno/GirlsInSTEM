WOMEN WHO CHANGED THE WORLD

Meet the STEM Queens 👑

They dared to dream. They worked. They changed history.

👩🏾‍🚀

Mae Jemison

Astronaut & Scientist

The first Black woman to travel into space. She proved that the stars are within reach.

👩🏻‍💻

Ada Lovelace

Computing Pioneer

Recognized for her early work on computer programming and imaginative vision of computing.

👩🏾‍🔬

Wangari Maathai

Scientist & Environmentalist

A Kenyan scientist who founded the Green Belt Movement and inspired environmental action.

👩🏻‍🔬

Mathematician

Her mathematical calculations helped NASA's early space missions succeed.
